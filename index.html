Company profile

Who we are

The company was founded in 2011, focusing on the R&D and sales of household personal care products, including but not limited to: hair straightener, facial cleansing devices, and nail grinders.

We have always upheld quality as our foundation, applying strict standards to every stage—from raw material selection to finished product delivery. We craft our products with dedication and run our business with integrity, committed to providing customers with truly reliable and trustworthy solutions.
